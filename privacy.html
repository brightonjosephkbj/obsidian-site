Privacy policy

Obsidian is made by B24 Technologies. Last updated November 6, 2026.

What we collect

  • Account details you give us, such as a username or email.
  • Your playlists, listening history and the songs you share.
  • Basic technical details: app version, Android version, device model, last time you used the app, and error reports.

How we use it

To run the app, sync your playlists and shares, limit downloads fairly, fix errors and send app announcements. We do not sell your data.

Third-party services

Obsidian gets music and information from services such as Audius, Jamendo and the Free Music Archive. Their own privacy policies apply when you use their content. Our servers are hosted with third-party providers.

Ads

Obsidian may show ads in the future. Ad providers may use an advertising ID on your device. If we add ads, we will update this page first.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your data at any time by emailing us. You can also uninstall the app at any time.

Children

Obsidian is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
