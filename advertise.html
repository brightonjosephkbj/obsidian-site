Advertise with us

Reach people who love music. Obsidian is a free music app, and your promotion appears right on the home screen where listeners start.

What you can promote

  • Hero slot: a full-width card at the top of the home screen with your title, a short line, artwork and a button that opens your link.
  • Banner: a smaller sponsored banner on the home screen.

Who it's for

  • Artists and producers with a new song or album.
  • Event promoters, clubs and DJs.
  • Local shops and businesses that want music fans to see them.

How it works

  • Email us what you want to promote and for how long.
  • We reply with the available slots and the rate.
  • You send your artwork, text and link, and we put it live.

Rates depend on the slot and how long it runs. Contact us and we will send them.
